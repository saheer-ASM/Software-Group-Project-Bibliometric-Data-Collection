## NM target and components — separate from baselines

The target is the **stored** `author_nm_index.nm_index`, loaded by stable author ID and marked `STORED_PRODUCTION_RESULT`; it is not recomputed here. Latest PDF §2.5 pp. 12–13, Eqs. 24–27, transforms six components, converts to percentiles, and combines `Nm_a = Σ_i w_i Per_a(x_i)` with `Σw_i=1`; equal sixths are proposed, not required. The actual stored percentile reference population and missing-component policy are not established by the manuscript. The pipeline reads six stored `per_*` percentiles and six raw component values from their respective tables; they never populate baseline columns.

| NM component | Latest-PDF source and operation | Stored authoritative input |
|---|---|---|
| `T_a` weighted normalized total impact | §2.4.1 p. 10 Eq. 11: career factor times field/year-normalized, author-weighted ISC-adjusted citation sum; unlike raw Total Cites. | `author_total_cites.total_cites_score`; percentile `author_nm_index.per_total_cites` |
| `S_a` weighted citations per paper | §2.4.2 pp. 10–11 Eqs. 12–13: field percentile cap, weighted ISC-adjusted citations divided by paper count and field mean, career factor. | `author_citations_per_paper.citations_per_paper_score`; percentile `per_citations_per_paper` |
| `U_a` weighted citation rate | §2.4.3 p. 11 Eq. 14: capped adjusted citations over years since paper publication and field/year mean, with contribution and career factor. | `author_citation_rate.citation_rate_score`; percentile `per_citation_rate` |
| `Hf′_a` modified fractional h | §2.4.4 p. 11 Eqs. 15–17: career/contribution-weighted adjusted effective citations, per-field h and field-weighted average. **Not conventional hf.** | `author_modified_hindex.modified_hindex_final`; percentile `per_modified_hf_index` |
| `Hm′_a` modified multi-author h | §2.4.5 pp. 11–12 Eqs. 18–20: effective rank sums career factor × author-field contribution; per-field score and weighted average. **Not conventional hm.** | `author.modified_hm_index`; percentile `per_modified_hm_index` |

Equation 12's field-percentile cap and Fig. 1/p. 14 IQR trimming disagree; the validation project does not select between them or recompute NM.
