## Bugs and corrections

The earlier registry and reports treated conventional hf as unresolved despite the latest PDF's uniform `1/N_p` worked calculation. A prior strategy-only `metrics/hf_index.py` could not produce the explicitly defined comparator. It now calculates `c_p/N_p` only when complete coauthor counts are verified; missing, invalid or conflicting author-paper input returns null. The registry, provenance, methodology status, documentation and synthetic fixture were updated accordingly. The real database's ten author slots do not prove complete `N_p`, so the real/pilot pipeline deliberately leaves hf null.

Specialized Fi and AR strategies previously lacked their own approval-reference check and could return unsupported numbers when called directly; both now enforce it. Generic and C-score strategy results now reject nonfinite values. The m-index helper now rejects invalid/nonfinite/negative duration. The mock annual fixture gained an explicitly synthetic hf time series so RQ6's existing all-comparator input contract continues to work. These changes are confined to this isolated project. Raw Total Cites, h and the written g formula needed no formula correction.

## Remaining methodology and input ambiguities

Seven baseline definitions/conventions remain unresolved as shown in [Phase 5A readiness](phase5a_readiness.md). The g example is inconsistent with the written finite-paper formula: five displayed papers totaling 165 imply `g=5` under `g≤5`, whereas the manuscript reports `g=12`; whether additional zero-cited papers or padding were intended is unstated. [The discrepancy analysis](g_index_source_issue.md) gives the manual calculation and decision needed. Conventional hf is formula-defined but cannot run for real authors until complete `N_p` is independently verified. Production author-paper linkage and `publication.total_citation` provenance remain real-data gates; schema columns alone do not establish complete portfolios.
