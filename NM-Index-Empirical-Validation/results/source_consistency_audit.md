# Latest-source consistency audit

Compared the complete [experiment design](../source_documents/Pasted%20markdown%287%29.md)
with the complete [latest manuscript](../source_documents/Nm%20Index%20novel%20model%20%281%29%283%29.pdf)
on 2026-10-04. These are statements in source materials, not measured findings.
The older PDF is excluded from this comparison.

Additional internal manuscript cautions from Appendix pp. 32–36: proof text
assumes `CF_com≥1` for the career factor although Eq. 10 with positive `λ`
eventually falls below one; Lemma 17 uses `r/(N+1)` whereas Eq. 24 specifies
the Blom position `(r−0.375)/(N+0.25)`; and several normalization proofs
assume per-author weights summing to one when Eq. 1 uses `1/Np` per author.
These are source inconsistencies, not validation-project calculations.
