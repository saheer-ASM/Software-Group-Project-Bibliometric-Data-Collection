The 11 baseline columns in `src/metric_registry.py` are distinct. `src/pipeline.py` constructs only `raw_total_cites`, conventional `h_index`, and conventional `g_index` by default. Its strategy path requires an explicit object and a nonempty source reference, but **does not establish that an external strategy was reviewed or approved**; the provenance CSV's `READY_WITH_EXPLICIT_STRATEGY` label can overstate readiness. Treat externally supplied strategy outputs as unapproved until a separate approval record, input-completeness check and test evidence exist. This is a data-execution blocker, not a reason to invent formulas here.

The NM target is read from `author_nm_index.nm_index` through `nm_index/loader.py`; it is **never recomputed**. The six raw component scores used for RQ10 come from `author_total_cites.total_cites_score`, `author_citations_per_paper.citations_per_paper_score`, `author_citation_rate.citation_rate_score`, `author_modified_hindex.modified_hindex_final`, `author.modified_hm_index`, and `modified_g_index_results.modified_g_index`. They are not conventional baseline Total Cites/hm/hf/g, and they are distinct from the six `author_nm_index.per_*` percentiles. The raw score availability and the final stored NM missing-component policy remain unresolved; [Phase 4.5](phase4_5_completion.md) found 784 scored authors missing at least one NM percentile.

`metrics/inputs.py` deduplicates stable author–paper pairs, refuses missing/nonfinite/negative citation counts, and leaves authors with no verified links unknown rather than zero. All ready calculators are deterministic over the prepared input. Offline tests cover five-paper counts, zeros, ties, missing counts, duplicate links, strategy rejection, the stored NM identity and a mocked pipeline. They do **not** validate an actual 100,000-author cohort, complete author lists, citation provenance, or externally supplied baseline strategies. The current database has only 816 linked publications among 56,768 publication rows, so full empirical baseline comparisons remain blocked.
